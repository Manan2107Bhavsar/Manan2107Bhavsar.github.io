## What it is

A pallet racking unit I modelled in Inventor 2027 for MANU 209, Product Manufacturing Drafting. The rack is made of two welded sub-assemblies: a beam weldment and an upright panel weldment. On top of the model I made a general arrangement, sub-assembly and part drawings, and a presentation file.

## What I did

- Modelled the beam weldment (PR-2026-100) from a beam and two plates.
- Modelled the upright panel weldment (PR-2026-200) from a base, a column and two braces.
- Put the two weldments together in the general arrangement (PR-2026-GA) with a parts list.
- Drew each part on its own sheet with a material list. The parts are mild steel, as listed on the sheets.
- Made a presentation file and used it for two exploded isometric views: a full explode and a simple one.

## Drawings

The set has 12 sheets. After the cover come the presentation sheet with the exploded views, the general arrangement, the beam weldment and its three parts (PR-2026-101 to 103), and the panel weldment and its four parts (PR-2026-201 to 204). Each sheet uses the same title block and part numbering, so any part can be traced from the general arrangement down to its own drawing.
