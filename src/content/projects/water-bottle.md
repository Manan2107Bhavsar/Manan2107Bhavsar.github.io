## What it is

A 5-gallon water jug. I modelled it in Inventor 2027 for MANU 209, Product Manufacturing Drafting. The assembly is the bottle body plus a cap assembly, and each part has its own drawing.

## What I did

- Modelled the bottle body and a decal for it.
- Built the cap assembly from three parts: the cap, a plug and a seal, plus a decal for the cap.
- Added a material list to each part sheet. The bottle, cap and plug are clear polycarbonate and the seal is rubber, as listed on the sheets.
- Used section views to show how the cap, plug and seal fit together, since you can't see that from the outside.
- Made exploded front and isometric views of both the full assembly and the cap assembly.

## Drawings

The set has 12 sheets. After the cover, two sheets cover the water bottle assembly (views, a section and the exploded views) and two cover the cap assembly. Then there is a detail drawing for each part: the bottle on two sheets, the bottle decal, the cap, the seal, the plug and the cap decal. Every sheet has a parts list or material list, so the set reads from the full jug down to the smallest part.
