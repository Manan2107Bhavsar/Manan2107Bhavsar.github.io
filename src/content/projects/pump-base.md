## What it is

This is the pump base project from MANU 209, Product Manufacturing Drafting. It is a general arrangement of a three-phase AC motor driving an end-suction centrifugal pump through a flexible shaft coupling. Both sit on a welded steel base, and the base sits on a concrete foundation. I modelled it in Inventor 2027 and made the full drawing set, plus a presentation file for the exploded views.

## What I did

- Built the base frame as a weldment from 11 plate parts: the base plate, front and rear side plates, motor-end and pump-end plates, a motor frame divider, the motor, pump and guard decks, and the guard risers.
- Modelled the concrete foundation as its own assembly, with a concrete pad and a grout pad.
- Made the shaft coupling assembly, a coupling guard (drawn with its flat pattern) and a pump support bracket.
- Placed the hardware: wedge anchors, leveling screws with square weld nuts, hex bolts, screws and nuts, flat and lock washers, slotted shims and a machine key. Purchased parts came from McMaster-Carr models, and the pump was a supplied model.
- Gave every part a number in one scheme: MHB-PB-000 for the general arrangement, the 100s for the base frame, the 200s for the foundation, and 300 to 500 for the coupling, guard and bracket.

## Drawings

The set has 23 sheets. After the cover come three general arrangement sheets: orthographic and isometric views with a 19-item parts list, an exploded isometric with balloons, and mounting details for the motor, pump, guard and base plate. Then the base frame weldment, a detail drawing for each plate, the foundation with its pad and grout, the coupling assembly, the guard and the bracket.
