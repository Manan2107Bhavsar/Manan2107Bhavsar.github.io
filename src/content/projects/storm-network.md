## What it is

Storm sewer design for Stage 6 of "Shatter Point", the subdivision we work on in CVEN 199. My part was to split Stage 6 into drainage areas and design the storm water pipe network that carries the runoff away. Everything in Stage 6 drains to the south-west corner, by the pond.

## What I did

- Divided Stage 6 into drainage areas in Civil 3D and measured the size of each one.
- Laid out the storm network: manholes, catch basins and the pipes between them.
- Built a storm sewer design spreadsheet in Excel using the course formulas, with rim and invert elevations for the structures.
- Worked out a trunk forecast for Stages 5 to 8 (the longest flow paths and the trunk flows) and shared it with a classmate.

## Drawings

The drawing is one sheet at 1:1000: the Stage 6 drainage areas and storm layout plan. It labels the manholes, catch basins, high and low points, and the area of each drainage area in hectares.

## What I learned

At my co-op I draft water and sewer pipe drawings. This project was the design side of that work: splitting the land into drainage areas, deciding where the water goes, and checking the network in a spreadsheet with the course formulas.
