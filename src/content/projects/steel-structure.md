## What it is

A steel connection I modelled for STRU 104, Structural Drafting. A W-shape column stands on a base plate, and two W-shape beams frame into it, held by top and seat angles and bolted together. The drawing set is titled "Steel Structure As-built". I put the assembly together in Inventor, with some parts made in SolidWorks.

## What I did

- Modelled the steel members: the W-shape column, two W-shape beams, the top and seat angles and a 10" x 14" base plate.
- Modelled the fasteners: A325 hex head screws, longer partially threaded hex head screws, hex nuts and stainless steel washers.
- Assembled everything and made an exploded isometric view with a parts list, so each piece is easy to pick out.
- Drew every member and fastener on its own sheet with front, side and isometric views and a material list.

## Drawings

The set has 11 sheets. After the cover come two assembly sheets: an exploded isometric and a sheet of front and side views, both with the parts list. Then there is one sheet each for the base plate, column, angle, beam, hex head screw, long hex head screw, hex nut and washer.

The fasteners are small, so their sheets are drawn at enlarged scales (up to 3:1) while the members are drawn at reduced scales.
