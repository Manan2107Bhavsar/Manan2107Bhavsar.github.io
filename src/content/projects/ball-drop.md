## What it is

A fun build, made out of curiosity and not for marks. A ball rolls off a ramp, bounces off a deflector (a backboard) and lands in a glass that stands on a base plate. I modelled the pieces in Inventor and ran the whole shot in Inventor's Dynamic Simulation.

## What I did

- Modelled the ramp, the ball, the deflector, the glass and the base plate, and put them together in one assembly.
- Set the assembly up in Dynamic Simulation, so Inventor works out how the ball moves instead of me animating it by hand.
- Ran the simulation to follow the ball from the ramp, off the backboard and into the glass.

## The model

The 3D model above is the assembly from the simulation: the ramp, the ball, the backboard, the glass and the base plate. Rotate it to see how the pieces line up for the shot.
