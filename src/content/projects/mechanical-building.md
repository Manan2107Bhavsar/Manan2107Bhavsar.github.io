## What it is

A mechanical model of a building, made in Revit for MECH 104, Industrial Building Mechanical Drafting 1. The mechanical model is linked to an architectural model, so the pipes, ducts and equipment are placed in the actual building. I built it on the course template, with shared parameters.

## In the 3D view

Ducts are green, hot water is orange-red, cold water is blue and sanitary piping is brown. The architectural walls are see-through and the roof is left out, so you can look inside and follow the systems.

## What I did

The model holds:

- 513 pipe segments and 401 pipe fittings
- 114 duct segments and 29 air terminals
- 22 pieces of mechanical equipment
- 15 plumbing fixtures
- 27 pipe accessories

The model uses families for floor cleanouts, sink and water closet rough-ins, and threaded ball valves.

For the course I also made a separate model for the final exam project, and wrote a project charter and a building workflow plan.
