## What it is

A structural model of a building that I made in Revit for STRU 104, Structural Drafting. The building has a concrete-and-steel frame, and the mechanical ductwork and piping are coordinated inside it. The cover sheet shows the whole thing as a 3D view, so you can see the structure and the mechanical systems together.

## What I did

- Modelled the structure in Revit, from the foundation up through the main floor to the roof.
- Showed the mechanical ductwork and piping coordinated inside the structure.
- Set up the views and sheets for the drawing set: plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules.

## Drawings

The set has 7 sheets on 24" x 36" (ARCH D) paper:

1. Cover with the 3D view of the building
2. Framing plans for the main floor and roof
3. North, south, east and west elevations
4. Section views
5. Typical details
6. Foundation plan
7. Schedules: grade beam, rebar and structural column

The framing plans and foundation plan show the structure level by level, and the schedules list the grade beams, rebar and structural columns used in the model.
